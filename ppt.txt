01
The Problem
Marginalized (SC) entrepreneurs eligible for
concessional loans (6.5%-8% ROI, income ≤ ₹5.00L)
cannot apply directly to the Ministry. They must
navigate a 3-tier system of 100+ Channel Partners
(SCAS, Banks, RRBs), resulting in severe scheme
confusion, misrouted applications, and months
wasted at branches choked by high NPAs or
exhausted fund quotas.
02
Our Solution
An intelligent, voice-first digital matchmaking platform
that bridges the citizen-to-channel divide. It takes
simple project parameters in local dialects,
automatically maps the user to the right credit line
(Microfinance vs. Term Loans), simulates subvention
EMIs with 3-12 month moratoriums, and routes them
to active, liquid partner branches.
03
What Makes It Different
Unlike generic directory lookups, we employ HealthAware Geo-Routing that evaluates branch NPA ratios
and unspent apex quotas to prevent pipeline deadends. Combined with multilingual Bhashini voice
assistance and pre-underwritten digital dossiers, we
eliminate intermediate agent dependency.

Novelty and Uniqueness
EXISTING SOLUTIONS & LIMITATIONS
myScheme Portal (Generic Aggregator)
Acts as an informational catalog only; provides no direct channel partner linkage or
live operational eligibility.
WHAT'S GENUINELY NEW (OUR INNOVATIONS)
Health-Aware Multi-Criteria Geo-Routing
Routes by Proximity + Partner Health (NPA ratio < threshold) + Live Quota Liquidity,
ensuring zero dead-end applications.
JanSamarth Portal (Commercial Lending Focus)
Tailored for standard commercial banks (MUDRA/PMEGP); fails to incorporate
specialized SCA channel finance & SC subventions.
Subvention-Specific Amortization Engine
Calculates 90:10 loan-to-margin splits, subsidized interest rates (6.5%-8%), and
custom 3-12 month moratorium grace periods.
Fragmented State Portals & Offline Middlemen
Static, text-heavy English/regional forms with zero visibility into branch liquidity;
high agent fee exploitation.
Voice-First Vernacular Intent Extraction
Bhashini speech-to-text pipeline enables semi-literate rural artisans to speak
naturally without knowing scheme acronyms.

Feasibility, Risks & Mitigation Strategy
A POTENTIAL CHALLENGES & RISKS
1. Vernacular Voice Accuracy & Dialects
Rural background noise and regional accents may degrade automated Speech-to-Text
extraction accuracу.
CONCRETE MITIGATION STRATEGIES
1. Hybrid Visual-Voice Fallback
Extracted numerical figures are displayed on high-contrast visual slider cards for instant
one-tap confirmation.
600 x400
2. Live Telemetry Gaps from State Agencies
Certain rural SCAs may lack real-time API feeds for live fund utilization and branch-level
overdue status.
2. Nodal Officer Admin Portal & Batch Sync
Provides a web-based MIS portal for district officers to batch-upload quarterly quotas, with
confidence-decay scoring.
3. Digital Literacy & Connectivity Divide
Target marginalized beneficiaries in remote areas may face low smartphone ownership and
weak 2G/3G data.
3. Assisted-Mode (CSC/VLE Portal)
Lightweight offline-capable PWA optimized for Village Level Entrepreneurs at Common
Service Centres to assist applicants.
